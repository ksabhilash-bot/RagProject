# AKS Pvt. Limited

## Company Overview

AKS Pvt. Limited is a leading information technology and digital transformation company specializing in software development, cloud solutions, and enterprise technology services. The company was founded on March 26, 2027, with the vision of helping businesses leverage modern technologies to improve efficiency, scalability, and customer experience.

Headquartered in Kochi, Kerala, India, AKS Pvt. Limited serves clients across various industries including healthcare, education, retail, logistics, finance, and manufacturing.

The company is committed to delivering high-quality software solutions while maintaining industry best practices in security, performance, and user experience.
